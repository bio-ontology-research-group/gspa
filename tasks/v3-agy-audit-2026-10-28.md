## 3. What this means for the open requirements

- Requirement 7 (retain weak candidates, distinguish roles) and 11 (agent vs simple
  methods) lack an independent truth set. The seven cases above are the seed of one:
  each has a complete candidate universe, a resolved active member, and explicit
  evidence-backed negatives (inactive paralog, regulator, editing subunit, different
  substrate). They are too few to fit anything; they are a **held-out test**, not
  training data.
- The training table labels evidence-less candidates `closed_world_unconfirmed = 0`.
  Cases like AAK40855.1 / EC 4.1.1.50 show that evidence-backed negatives exist and
  should carry a distinct `label_basis` (e.g. `negative_truth`), so that
  within-locus ranking metrics can be computed on evidence rather than on absence.
- Genomic-context bonuses in the legacy integration rewarded the wrong paralog in
  the GuaB case (context 0.167 on GuaB1 for IMPDH). This is exactly the "naive
  additive context" problem the plan's item 4/5 set out to replace with learned
  weights; it should be a regression test for the v3 integration.
