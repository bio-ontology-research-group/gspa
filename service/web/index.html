Follow the biological evidence

  1. Find candidate functionsDeepGO++ Light predicts GO functions. Reviewed Swiss-Prot homologs supply enzyme evidence and optional protein names, with the source recorded.
  2. Apply biological contextCombine functions with genomic neighbours, KEGG pathways, AMR, biosynthetic clusters and localization. Inspect the reconstructed metabolic model and its fluxes.
  3. Investigate a biological questionSearch a process such as “threonine biosynthesis”, find candidate proteins, and inspect their genomic neighbours when coordinates are available.

A predicted function is a hypothesis. Scores help prioritise candidates; they do not establish experimental function or prove pathway completeness. Proteins without a close homolog may receive no prediction.

DeepGO++ Light uses a 0.5 prediction threshold; integrated scores may differ. The CPU workflow includes DIAMOND, AMRFinderPlus, antiSMASH, PSORTb, annotation quality and bacterial FBA. Organism and coordinate requirements are reported explicitly. Annotation quality is distinct from assembly completeness or contamination.
