\section{Extended Suite 1 and Suite 2 benchmarks}
\label{sec:supp_suite12}

Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_holdout125} reports the complete 16-row post-cutoff Swiss-Prot enzyme benchmark (\texttt{Holdout-125}, $n=125$ proteins across 293 ground-truth protein--EC pairs; source: \texttt{results/scenario1\_swissprot\_ec\_holdout125\_real.tsv}), separating standalone per-protein baselines, single-predictor refinement (\texttt{DeepProZyme + METEOR v8} and \texttt{DeepProZyme + GSPA Single-Pred}), and 3-method ensemble pooling (\texttt{Noisy-OR} and \texttt{GSPA Iter 1--3}). Because these 125 proteins are dispersed across 74 distinct species and evaluated as isolated sequences, single-predictor and ensemble refinement raise Top-3 recall to $43.2\%$ ($54/125$) and Top-5 recall to $44.0\%$ ($55/125$) while preserving Top-1 accuracy ($32.0\%$--$33.6\%$ vs.\ $32.8\%$ for standalone CLEAN). Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_95genomes} reports the complete 12-row enzyme evaluation across the 95-genome bacterial panel under four donor-database homology-masking tiers (\texttt{vanilla}, $\le 70\%$, $\le 50\%$, and $\le 30\%$ sequence identity; source: \texttt{results/scenario1\_95genomes\_ec\_homology\_real.tsv}, generated by \texttt{scripts/eval\_scenario1\_holdouts\_real.py:340--375}), comparing raw METEOR v8 top-1 flips against GSPA's single-predictor stoichiometric/pathway acceptance filter (\texttt{GSPA Single-Pred}, \texttt{blind\_single\_accept}: accepts a candidate METEOR flip $b_1 \to m_1$ when $m_1$ maps to at least one elementally balanced universal reaction and either shares the 3-digit EC sub-subclass with $b_1$ at $\ge$ KEGG pathway count or shares the 1-digit EC class with strictly higher KEGG pathway count and $\ge$ balanced reaction count) and GSPA's 3-channel consensus acceptance filter (\texttt{GSPA Multi-Pred}, \texttt{blind\_accept\_3ch}: accepts a candidate flip when $m_1$ receives strictly greater support than $b_1$ across the other two predictors' base and METEOR top-1 calls), alongside exact discordant protein counts ($b_{01}, b_{10}$) and two-sided exact McNemar $p$-values.

\section{Stage 3 neuro-symbolic agent tool schemas, multi-seed stability, and extremophile case study}
\label{sec:supp_agent}

Stage 3 provides five deterministic, read-only genomic and ontological inspection tools to the open-weight instruction model \texttt{Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8} (\texttt{benchmark/agent/run\_gspa\_agent.py}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \texttt{inspect\_operon\_neighborhood(protein\_id, window=5)}: Returns the de-identified genomic neighborhood ($\pm 5$ genes on the same contig), intergenic distances, strand orientation, and neighbor GO/EC annotations.
  \item \texttt{inspect\_pathway\_or\_complex\_gap(anomaly\_id)}: Returns the target pathway or heteromeric complex identifier, fraction of partner steps/subunits present in the genome at $P \ge 0.50$, and list of missing steps/subunits.
  \item \texttt{query\_protein\_evidence(protein\_id)}: Returns Stage 0 per-channel calibrated probabilities, Pfam/InterPro structural domain annotations, and precomputed RLGC kernel density Bayes factors ($\text{BF}_{\text{operon}}$).
  \item \texttt{check\_symbolic\_constraints(go\_or\_ec\_term, taxon\_id)}: Queries the deterministic ontology bridge ($26{,}911$ direct GO taxon rules and $2{,}715$ GO--Uberon/PO/CL anatomical rules linked to $8{,}331$ taxon constraints [$4{,}767$ \texttt{only\_in\_taxon}, $3{,}564$ \texttt{never\_in\_taxon}] across $7{,}722$ anatomical and $1{,}818$ taxonomic hierarchy edges) and returns whether the term is valid in \texttt{taxon\_id}, along with its nearest valid non-root GO ancestors if forbidden.
  \item \texttt{propose\_resolution(action, target\_protein\_id, resolved\_term, confidence, justification)}: Submits the final structured decision (\texttt{PROMOTE}, \texttt{RELAX}, \texttt{DEMOTE}, or \texttt{ABSTAIN}), which is deterministically verified by \texttt{SymbolicGuardrail} before acceptance.
\end{enumerate}
