\section{Hyperparameter Specification and One-at-a-Time Sensitivity Analysis}
\label{sec:supp_hyperparams}

All 28 pipeline constants across Stage 1 (iterative Bayesian log-odds refiner), Stage 2A (deterministic symbolic enforcer), and Stage 2B (stoichiometric locus refiner) were fitted exclusively on the \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 development proteome via two-stage block coordinate search and held frozen across all out-of-sample prokaryotic, archaeal, viral, and eukaryotic benchmarks. Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_hyperparams} reports the complete parameter inventory, default values, and one-at-a-time $\pm 20\%$ perturbation sensitivity across the 8 out-of-sample reference genomes of Table~1, the 144 held-out metabolic reactions in \textit{Bacillus subtilis} 168 ($76$ covered by candidate pools), and the 22-genome \texttt{Price-149} RB-TnSeq enzyme benchmark (source: \texttt{results/hyperparameter\_sensitivity\_non\_ecoli.tsv}). Across all 28 parameters, a $\pm 20\%$ perturbation changes the 8-genome out-of-sample micro $F_{\max}$ by at most $0.0008$, the internal GAEF composite score by at most $0.0038$, \textit{B.~subtilis} LRO Hit@1 by at most $1.32$ percentage points, and \texttt{Price-149} CLEAN single-predictor $F_{\max}$ ($0.6084$) by at most $0.0042$.

\section{Stage 3 Neuro-Symbolic Agent Tool Schemas, Multi-Seed Stability, and Extremophile Case Study}
\label{sec:supp_agent}

Stage 3 provides five deterministic, read-only genomic and ontological inspection tools to the open-weight instruction model \texttt{Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8} (\texttt{benchmark/agent/run\_gspa\_agent.py}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \texttt{inspect\_operon\_neighborhood(protein\_id, window=5)}: Returns the de-identified genomic neighborhood ($\pm 5$ genes on the same contig), intergenic distances, strand orientation, and neighbor GO/EC annotations.
  \item \texttt{inspect\_pathway\_or\_complex\_gap(anomaly\_id)}: Returns the target pathway or heteromeric complex identifier, fraction of partner steps/subunits present in the genome at $P \ge 0.50$, and list of missing steps/subunits.
  \item \texttt{query\_protein\_evidence(protein\_id)}: Returns Stage 0 per-channel calibrated probabilities, Pfam/InterPro structural domain annotations, and precomputed RLGC kernel density Bayes factors ($\text{BF}_{\text{operon}}$).
  \item \texttt{check\_symbolic\_constraints(go\_or\_ec\_term, taxon\_id)}: Queries the deterministic ontology bridge ($26{,}911$ direct GO taxon rules and $2{,}715$ GO--Uberon/PO/CL anatomical rules linked to $8{,}331$ taxon constraints across $7{,}722$ anatomical and $1{,}818$ taxonomic hierarchy edges) and returns whether the term is valid in \texttt{taxon\_id}, along with its nearest valid non-root GO ancestors if forbidden.
  \item \texttt{propose\_resolution(action, target\_protein\_id, resolved\_term, confidence, justification)}: Submits the final structured decision (\texttt{PROMOTE}, \texttt{RELAX}, \texttt{DEMOTE}, or \texttt{ABSTAIN}), which is deterministically verified by \texttt{SymbolicGuardrail} before acceptance.
\end{enumerate}
