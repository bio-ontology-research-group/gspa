\subsection{Stage 0 Platt calibration parameters and correlation groups}

In Stage 0 (\texttt{ScoreCalibrator.groovy} and \texttt{LikelihoodModel.groovy}), raw predictor scores $s(c) \in [0, 1]$ are mapped via Platt scaling $\tilde{q}(c) = \text{clip}(\sigma_{\text{log}}(a_e s(c) + b_e), 10^{-4}, 0.9999)$ and multiplied by channel reliability $r_e \in (0, 1]$. Default calibration slopes and intercepts $(a_e, b_e)$ are $(7.0, -3.5)$ for sequence alignment (DIAMOND/BLAST), $(6.5, -2.9)$ for profile HMMs (HMMER/Pfam), $(6.0, -2.4)$ for InterProScan and KofamScan, $(6.8, -3.2)$ for ESM-2 and ProtT5 $k$-NN transfer, $(6.2, -2.8)$ for ProteInfer, DeepGOWeb, and DeepGO-SE, and $(6.4, -3.0)$ for FoldSeek and ProstT5 3Di structural transfer. Default channel reliability weights $r_e$ (\texttt{EvidenceType.groovy}) are $0.85$ for AMR domain predictors, $0.80$ for sequence homology and orthology, $0.75$ for profile HMMs and sequence domains, $0.70$ for protein-sequence ML, PLM $k$-NN, and structural similarity, $0.65$ for structure-based deep learning, $0.60$ for metabolic pathway context and inferred context, $0.55$ for genomic language models, and $0.45$ for raw genomic neighborhood context. Claims are partitioned into 11 correlation groups in \texttt{LikelihoodModel.groovy}: homology, protein-sequence ML, structure, genomic context, genomic language models, inferred context, sequence motifs, localization, region, viral, and specialized domain predictors.

\section{Secondary metabolism, metagenomic dark matter, and Stage 2B stoichiometric refinement}
\label{sec:supp_fba_bacdive}

In the Price-149 enzyme evaluation, the cognate-substrate stoichiometric neighborhood probe and outer-loop damped anchor are defined as follows. For each candidate pair $(p, u)$, let $n_1(p, u)$ and $n_2(p, u)$ count chromosomal neighbors within $\pm 10$ genes whose reactions share non-currency metabolites with $u$ at 1 and 2 hops, $f_{\text{op}}(p)$ denote the co-directional operon fraction, and $s_{\text{nb}}(p, u) = \min(2.0, \sum_{|g' - g| \le 10} (1 + 0.25|g' - g|)^{-1} P_{\text{cand}}(g', u))$ measure distance-weighted neighbor EC support. The local stoichiometric probe shift $\Delta L_{\text{probe}}(p, u) = 0.85 \min(1.5, 0.55 n_1 + 0.20 n_2) + 0.45 f_{\text{op}}(p)\mathbb{I}[n_1 + n_2 > 0] + 0.35\mathbb{I}[u \in \mathcal{R}_{\text{bal}}] - 0.50\mathbb{I}[u \notin \mathcal{R}_{\text{ec}}]$ and Stage 1 neighbor coherence shift $\Delta L_{\text{stage1}}(p, u) = 0.90 s_{\text{nb}}(p, u) + 0.50 f_{\text{op}}(p)\mathbb{I}[s_{\text{nb}}(p, u) > 0.20] + 0.30\mathbb{I}[u \in \mathcal{W}_{\text{kegg}}]$ are applied over $T=3$ damped outer iterations ($\lambda_t \in \{0.70, 0.42, 0.252\}$):
\begin{equation}
  P_{\text{single}}^{(t)}(p, u) = \sigma_{\text{log}}\!\Bigl(0.75 \, \text{logit}\bigl(P^{(t-1)}(p, u)\bigr) + 0.25 \, \text{logit}\bigl(P^{(0)}(p, u)\bigr) + \lambda_t \bigl(\Delta L_{\text{stage1}}(p, u) + 0.65\,\Delta L_{\text{probe}}(p, u)\bigr)\Bigr),
  \label{eq:supp_outer_anchor}
\end{equation}
and the multi-predictor posterior blends $0.55 P_{\text{single}}^{(t)}(p, u) + 0.45 P_{\text{NoisyOR}}(p, u)$. Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_bgc} reports the biosynthetic gene cluster benchmark across $106$ MIBiG/antiSMASH clusters, Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_97mags} reports the $97$-MAG uncultured microbial dark-matter panel across $16$ phyla, and Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_bigg} reports the 108-genome stoichiometric invariant audit, the comparison across six manually curated BiGG reference genome-scale metabolic models, and the role-stratified Price-149 breakdown on CLEAN.

\section{Stage 3 neuro-symbolic tool schemas, multi-seed stability, prospective CAFA6, and extremophile case study}
\label{sec:supp_agent}

Stage 3 provides five deterministic, read-only genomic and ontological inspection tools to the open-weight instruction model (\texttt{run\_gspa\_agent.py}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \texttt{inspect\_operon\_neighborhood(protein\_id, window=5)}: Returns the de-identified genomic neighborhood ($\pm 5$ genes on the same contig), intergenic distances, strand orientation, and neighbor GO/EC annotations.
  \item \texttt{inspect\_pathway\_or\_complex\_gap(anomaly\_id)}: Returns the target pathway or heteromeric complex identifier, fraction of partner steps or subunits present in the genome at $P \ge 0.50$, and list of missing steps or subunits.
  \item \texttt{query\_protein\_evidence(protein\_id)}: Returns Stage 0 per-channel calibrated probabilities, Pfam/InterPro structural domain annotations, and precomputed RLGC kernel density Bayes factors ($\text{BF}_{\text{operon}}$).
  \item \texttt{check\_symbolic\_constraints(go\_or\_ec\_term, taxon\_id)}: Queries the deterministic ontology bridge ($26{,}911$ direct GO taxon rules and $2{,}715$ GO--Uberon/PO/CL anatomical rules linked to $8{,}331$ taxon constraints across $7{,}722$ anatomical and $1{,}818$ taxonomic hierarchy edges) and returns whether the term is valid in \texttt{taxon\_id}, along with its nearest valid non-root GO ancestors if forbidden.
  \item \texttt{propose\_resolution(action, target\_protein\_id, resolved\_term, confidence, justification)}: Submits the final structured decision (\textsc{promote}, \textsc{relax}, \textsc{demote}, or \textsc{abstain}), which is deterministically verified by the symbolic guardrail before acceptance.
\end{enumerate}
