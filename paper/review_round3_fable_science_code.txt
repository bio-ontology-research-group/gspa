# Independent review of GSPA v2.1.0 (paper/main.tex) for Nature Methods

Conducted against the working tree of 2026-10-15 (HEAD 326c112 plus 103 uncommitted changes). Six subsystem audits were run and every consequential finding below was re-verified by me directly in the code or data.

## Executive summary and overall technical verdict

**Verdict: not acceptable in its current form.** Revisions to the code, the Methods equations, the benchmark designs, and the statistical analysis are all required, and most headline results must be regenerated before the paper can be assessed on its merits.

The programme is sound and timely, and parts of the implementation are real and well engineered: the SAT4J minimal-unsatisfiable-subset extraction, the relax/remove enforcer, the pFBA sidecar, and the live Qwen agent runs. But the manuscript does not describe the software that produced the numbers, and a large share of the numbers were not produced by the software at all. Three problems recur across all eight suites.

**The Methods describe algorithms that are not implemented.** Stage 0 has no isotonic calibration, no evidence-code weights and no donor-correlation exponent; channels collapse by max within three groups. Stage 1 uses an undocumented 0.5 damping, 6 iterations, a mean-delta stop, no propagation, and none of the gates or constants given for B1 to B6; the coherence weight has the opposite dependence on coverage to Eq. B2. Stage 2A has no gamma_relax and no 0.45 singleton attenuation. Stage 2B solves one LP, not two-step pFBA; the imbalance penalty is dead code; Eq. 7 outer damping does not exist. The BacDive formulas are not what the script computes. The Stage 3 guardrail loads zero direct taxon constraints because of a column-count bug.

**Several benchmarks are decided by construction.** The 120-anomaly, LRO-50 and LSO-M4 inputs are generated from the truth label. The eukaryotic anatomy evaluation adds the developmental-process ancestor closure to every perturbed protein's truth and counts violations against GSPA's own E4 ledger. The 108-genome invariant audit is two LP solves on random reaction probabilities replicated 108 times. Price-149 "cognate substrate" is a function of the ground-truth EC, and "Full GSPA on one predictor" pools four predictors. The 95-genome and Holdout-125 GSPA rows are additive heuristics on METEOR's flips. The 3,100-genome script draws baseline scores at random and boosts truth terms.

**Provenance and statistics are not auditable.** Eleven cited results files have no generating script, four tables are Python literals, nothing in results/ except the pillar1_2 ablation is under version control, the 1,000-genome and 13/21-panel SOTA tables contradict the only raw data in the repository, no Wilcoxon or McNemar p-value reproduces, the per-fold CV table is a fixed multiplicative pattern, the constraint-table counts are wrong, 15 of 304 core tests fail in the SAT checker, and release tag v2.1.0 does not exist (build is 2.0.0).

**What survives:** Table 1 cells, the 88-proteome domain means, the BacDive blind binary rows, the Price-149 and LRO table cells, the agent recall/precision counts and the runtime scaling table all match their TSVs and regenerate where a real script exists. The honest result is that genome-scale priors remove constraint violations and improve structural coherence while leaving protein-centric accuracy unchanged or slightly lower. That is publishable if the paper says so and the synthetic and leaking benchmarks are rebuilt.

## Part I: Scientific contributions, SOTA comparison and experimental design

**[S1] The central accuracy claim hides direction.** From `results/pillar1_2_ablation_per_genome.tsv`, CAFA Fmax A1 to A9 falls in 8 of 9 genomes (exact Wilcoxon p = 0.129, not 0.41) and experimental-only micro Fmax falls (0.2853 to 0.2847). **[S4] Benchmarks decided by construction.** (a) `run_pillar3_eukaryotic_anatomy.py:401-403` adds the closures of GO:0048856 and GO:0032502 to every perturbed protein's truth, exactly where relaxation lands; lines 752-769 take the violation oracle from GSPA's own E4 ledger, so E4 = 0 is tautological and E2 is never run; Fmax and IC are computed on at most 300 perturbed proteins.
