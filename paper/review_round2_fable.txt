## 1. Summary of Round 1 resolution and current recommendation

The revision is a substantial and largely good-faith response. The authors added the omitted controls (PLM-centroid-only LRO row, Noisy-OR row on Holdout-125, positive-cost LP in the audit), rewrote the abstract so Stage 0 and Stages 1–3 are credited separately, added non-E. coli subsets to Tables 1, 5 and 7, specified the hybrid and Track 2 protocols, wrote out all six priors, fixed the Noisy-OR discount, disclosed the METEOR authorship overlap, corrected the direction of the METEOR effect on the 50,075-pair panel, added intervals and paired tests in most places, and added a nomenclature table (Table 8), a provenance map, availability statements, contributions, competing interests, and the journal structure. Thirteen of my 15 Round 1 major comments are fully or substantially addressed. Two are not: Comment 1 (numerical provenance) and Comment 9a (endogenous-only eukaryotic evaluation).

The fresh audit found three new problems as serious as anything in Round 1, all verifiable from the deposited files. First, the Stage 3 benchmark hands the language model the answer: true candidates carry their real gene symbol and an annotation restating the target function, all 36 decoys share the symbol `rpoZ_paralog`, and the deposited agent rationales cite this text. The records are templated synthetic objects, not extracted anomalies. Second, the 1,000-genome RefSeq F_max column is numerically identical, aspect by aspect, to the CAFA temporal holdout file. Third, five "canonical" result files named in the manuscript, Table 8 and `PROVENANCE_MAP.md` do not exist, and the figure scripts still hard-code numbers, including an *A. aeolicus* benchmark that appears nowhere in the text. In addition, Table 6's information-content column, Table 1's out-of-sample row, Table 5's out-of-sample row, several Table 2 cells and about ten in-text numbers do not match their attributed files.

**Recommendation: major revision (second round).** The scientific core is sound and the two ideas I singled out in Round 1 remain good. But a paper whose claims rest on some forty numbers cannot go to production while files, figures and text still disagree, and the Stage 3 result cannot be published in its present form.
